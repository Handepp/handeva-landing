# PRD — Handeva Digital Product Hub Landing Page

## 1. Product Overview

### Product Name
Handeva

### Product Type
One-page digital product showcase / product hub website.

### Purpose
Handeva is the central landing page for showcasing digital products and web applications built by Handvea.

The website allows users to:
- understand Handvea briefly
- discover available products
- access each product website

Handeva is a digital product hub, not an agency website.

---

## 2. Goals

### Primary Goals

- Product discovery
- Easy access to digital products
- Build Handvea brand identity as an independent digital product studio

---

## 3. Non Goals

This website is NOT intended for:
- selling development services
- client portfolio showcase
- blog/content publishing
- long company profile pages

---

## 4. User Journey

User opens handeva.com

↓

Sees Handvea brand and message

↓

Browses digital products

↓

Clicks product

↓

Redirects to product website

---

## 5. Page Structure

Constraint:
- Single viewport experience
- No scrolling on desktop
- All important information visible immediately
